How to Use ChitManager

Step-by-step documentation for group organizers

1. Create a Chit Group

Tap "+ New Chit" to set your total chit value (e.g. ₹1,00,000), total months/cycles (e.g. 10), organizer commission %, monthly due date, and your UPI ID for WhatsApp reminders.

2. Add Members & Ticket Shares

Go to the Members tab and add participants with their WhatsApp numbers. Set Ticket Share to 1 for a full ticket, 0.5 for a half ticket, or 2 for a double ticket.

3. Conduct Monthly Draw / Auction (3 Modes)

  • Auction Chit: Select the winning bidder and enter the bid discount. The app automatically deducts your commission, splits the remaining discount as dividend per ticket, and updates everyone's net payable due.
  • Lottery / Draw Chit: Spin the digital draw wheel to randomly pick a winner strictly from members who have not won yet.
  • Fixed Turn Chit: Assign or pre-plan which member receives the pot in each month.

4. Track Collections & Share on WhatsApp

Tap any member's status badge to cycle between Pending, Paid, and Overdue. Use "Remind" to send an individual WhatsApp payment link, or broadcast the monthly result and pending list to your group.

5. Offline PWA & Backup

All data stays 100% locally on your device. Tap the database icon in the top bar anytime to download a .JSON backup file or restore it on another device.
